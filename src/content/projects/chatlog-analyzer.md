Built for a mid-sized Finnish company as my thesis. I interviewed the client to understand their needs, planned the work in an agile way, and implemented the app myself. It groups chatbot messages into service categories by keywords and shows the results as charts and downloadable reports. The demo data is anonymized. It is a proof of concept, a base for a possible production version.
