    Chatlog Analyzer

    Completed

    My thesis project, a proof-of-concept web app that shows where a company's internal chatbot has knowledge gaps and where staff might need more training. I interviewed the client, planned the work in an agile way and built everything myself. Python and pandas sort chatbot messages into service categories, and a Flask interface presents the results as Matplotlib charts and downloadable reports. The demo data is anonymized.

    My role: Solo thesis project, from client interviews to planning and development.
